**Representative model outcomes (do not extrapolate to an actual project):**
WP2 finds a positive E+S incremental annual hydrogen-delivery contrast relative
to E alone of approximately 343.79 tonnes in the original 2023 17-case
registered matrix. The original five-year WP4 Open-Meteo screening retains a
positive E+S interaction in all five years but does **not** make them
statistically independent samples. The observed-price E+S versus E
comparison changes net wholesale settlement by approximately
**+EUR 1.759 million**. That figure excludes investment, financing,
operations, fees, hydrogen revenue and market constraints.

## Explicitly incomplete work — not implied by the freeze label

1. **Physical external validity:** no plant telemetry or independent
   vendor-calibrated wind/electrolyser/storage model; one illustrative North
   Sea wind-grid location and screening assumptions only.
2. **Economics:** no independently sourced CAPEX/OPEX complete boundary,
   contractual power purchase, renewable-hydrogen eligibility or actual LCOH.
   Grid settlement is not an economic viability calculation.
3. **Engineering sensitivity:** [WP4 registered engineering sensitivity](WP4_ENGINEERING_SENSITIVITY.md)
   lists a further 17 one-factor/reference operating-assumption cases;
   its full-year outcome remains **not completed/admitted** in this frozen
   evidence milestone. This freeze does not silently convert 24-hour smoke
   runs into annual validation.
4. **Raw-source continuity:** recovered 2023 CDS NetCDF monthly redelivery
   values align but **raw bytes differ** from the original source hashes.
   The original 2023 CDS data are not interchangeable with WP4 Open-Meteo
   ERA5 despite close annual energy totals.
5. **Manuscript status:** no peer review, independent field replication,
   journal publication, formal research DOI or permanent archival guarantee.
6. **Remaining archival debt:** original source/experiment Actions artifacts
   have finite retention. The CDS NetCDF redelivery ZIP was **separately
   copied to Google Drive**:
   https://drive.google.com/file/d/1vJ0vfWom8Jgzdz6QCTEJB1hXHkPJnqN0/view?usp=drivesdk .
   A separate durable, integrity-verified deposit of the complete audit,
   source XML and all registered original hourly experiment outputs is
   **not confirmed**. Keep the hash manifests and collect the evidence
   before its artifact retention expires.

## How the project will be maintained after the freeze

- `main` stays available and CI remains active; the repository is **not
  archived, locked or made read-only**.
- **Default: no new UI/features, additional unconstrained scenarios or
  speculative findings.** Redirect new research capacity to separate projects.
- Allow only documentation/citation corrections, source preservation,
  security/dependency maintenance, reproducibility repairs or corrections
  to materially wrong results. Such changes require a reviewable PR,
  checks, a change log, and either a new frozen revision or explicit notice
  that the original snapshot has not changed.
- No alteration of saved results to improve headline metrics; future new
  hypotheses or scope extensions start a separately labelled study/release.
- If used in a CV, interview or research portfolio, cite the exact commit,
  characterize it as an **independent, non-peer-reviewed computational
  screening study**, and disclose the above empirical/economic limits.
